Season 2

Episode 14: The Mion River Battle

The battle against Caster concludes, but victory comes at a cost. Kiritsugu, using a Command Spell, forces Lancer's Master to have Lancer commit suicide. This ruthless act secures an advantage but disgusts Saber, deepening the rift between them.

Episode 15: The Golden Radiance

Following Lancer's demise, Kiritsugu coldly eliminates Kayneth and his fiancée. Meanwhile, Kirei Kotomine reveals his true nature by betraying and murdering his own master, Tokiomi Tohsaka, seizing control of his Servant, Archer.

Episode 16: The End of Honor

Kariya Matou, seeking to save Sakura, confronts Aoi Tohsaka, leading to a tragic misunderstanding that leaves her broken. Kariya's own body and mind continue to deteriorate under the strain of his magic.

Episode 17: The Eighth Servant

As the war nears its climax, the remaining Masters maneuver for the final confrontation. Kirei and Kiritsugu, two men defined by emptiness and ideals, prepare to face each other at last.

Episode 18 & 19: Distant Memories & Where Justice is Found

A two-part flashback reveals Kiritsugu's traumatic past, detailing the events that forged him into the "Magus Killer" and his desperate, tragic quest to become a hero who can save the world.

Episode 20: The Assassin's Return

The final battles begin. Rider challenges Archer to a duel between kings, unleashing his ultimate Noble Phantasm, Ionioi Hetairoi, a reality marble containing his entire army.

Episode 21: The Knight on Two Wheels

Berserker's identity is revealed as Sir Lancelot, once the greatest of the Knights of the Round Table. He engages Saber in a heartbreaking duel, forcing her to confront the failures and regrets of her past reign.

Episode 22: All the World's Evils

Rider is defeated by Archer's overwhelming power, but his young Master, Waver, survives, having earned the King's respect. Kiritsugu finally reaches the Holy Grail, only to discover its true, corrupt, and malevolent nature.

Episode 23: The Sea of Corruption

Kiritsugu vehemently rejects the Grail's twisted version of his wish. To prevent a catastrophe, he uses his remaining Command Spells to order Saber to destroy the Grail, unleashing a devastating wave of destruction upon Fuyuki City.

Episode 24: The Last Command Spell

In the blazing aftermath of the Grail's destruction, a broken Kiritsugu wanders the ruins and finds a single survivor: a young boy named Shirou Emiya. By saving him, Kiritsugu finds a sliver of redemption.

Episode 25: Epilogue

Years after the war, the survivors move on. Waver Velvet returns to Fuyuki to honor his king's memory, while Kirei Kotomine watches over Rin Tohsaka. The tragic conclusion of the Fourth Holy Grail War sets the stage for the events of *Fate/stay night*.
