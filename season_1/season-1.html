Season 1

Episode 1: Summoning of the Heroes

The Fourth Holy Grail War begins as mages from around the world prepare for the clandestine conflict. Kiritsugu Emiya, a notorious magus killer, is chosen as the Einzbern family's representative. Servants, heroic spirits from history, are summoned, setting the stage for a brutal battle of ideals and ambitions.

Episode 2: The Fake First Shot

Kirei Kotomine, a priest acting as the war's overseer and a Master himself, sends his Servant, Assassin, to probe the other participants. Tokiomi Tohsaka, a powerful magus, reveals his own Servant: the arrogant and overwhelmingly powerful Archer, Gilgamesh.

Episode 3: Fuyuki City

Waver Velvet, a young and insecure student from the Clock Tower, successfully summons the boisterous Rider, Iskandar, the King of Conquerors. Together, they begin their unorthodox campaign in Fuyuki City, with Rider's charisma clashing with Waver's cautious nature.

Episode 4: Blade of the Demonic Spear

The first major Servant battle erupts as Lancer engages Saber at the docks. Their duel is a spectacular display of skill and honor, but it is dramatically interrupted by the arrival of Rider, who seeks to recruit them into his army.

Episode 5: A Vicious Roar

Rider's grand entrance forces the other Servants—Saber, Lancer, and Archer—to reveal themselves. Just as tensions escalate, a mysterious black knight, Berserker, appears and launches a frenzied, targeted assault on Saber.

Episode 6: A Night of Schemes

The Masters regroup and plot their next moves after the chaotic first encounter. Kirei Kotomine grows increasingly fascinated by Kiritsugu Emiya's ruthless ideology, while Kariya Matou struggles with the immense physical toll of controlling the unstable Berserker.

Episode 7: The Evil Forest

The sadistic Caster and his equally deranged Master, Ryunosuke, establish a gruesome workshop in the city's sewers, kidnapping and murdering children for their own amusement. Saber and her proxy master, Irisviel, begin investigating the disappearances.

Episode 8: The Magus Killer

Kiritsugu demonstrates his brutal pragmatism by attacking Lancer's Master, Kayneth Archibald, not with magic, but with modern weaponry and cunning tactics. The assault leaves the proud magus crippled and humiliated.

Episode 9: Master and Servant

The relationship between Waver and Rider deepens as Rider imparts his kingly wisdom upon his young Master. Their bond is tested as they confront other Masters and debate the nature of their own ambitions for the Grail.

Episode 10: Rin's Adventure

In a side story, a young Rin Tohsaka, daughter of Tokiomi, embarks on her own secret mission to find a missing friend, showcasing her prodigious talent and getting a glimpse into the dark world of magic.

Episode 11: The Grail Dialogue

Rider hosts a "Banquet of Kings," inviting Saber and Archer to join him in a philosophical debate. The three kings clash over their vastly different ideals of kingship and their reasons for seeking the Holy Grail.

Episode 12: The Grail's Invitation

Caster's atrocities reach a terrifying climax as he summons a colossal sea monster in the Mion River, threatening the entire city. The Masters are forced into a temporary and fragile alliance to combat the immense threat.

Episode 13: The Forbidden Banquet

The Servants combine their Noble Phantasms in a desperate battle against Caster's monster. The conflict culminates with Saber unleashing her ultimate attack, Excalibur, in a brilliant flash of light that obliterates the creature and ends the first half of the war.
